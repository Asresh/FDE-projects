## Design implications

Across the reviewed roles, the repeated expectation is practical customer delivery: understand a consequential workflow, connect real systems, build an end-to-end solution, make operational risks visible, and leave behind a usable pattern. PayoutProof applies that shape to marketplace finance operations. It demonstrates two source-system adapters (processor event API and bank statement CSV), explicit financial matching rules, durable evidence, human review, and an outbound notification integration. It avoids floating-point money calculations and does not claim that a mismatch proves fraud or loss.
