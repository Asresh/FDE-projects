## Synthesis

These roles describe an engineer who discovers a customer workflow, integrates the systems that hold its evidence, builds an end-to-end solution, carries it through production readiness and adoption, and returns field learning as reusable product patterns. The technical thread combines software engineering, data normalization, customer-specific integration, system design, operational reliability, measurable outcomes, and clear communication. Compensation varies by role, level, geography, and equity; posted salary bands are not directly comparable.

## Project choices derived from that research

ColdChain Guard demonstrates a new domain relative to this repository's incident-triage and AI-readiness projects: distribution quality operations. It uses a sensor-gateway HTTP boundary, a relational inventory adapter, a stateful policy/case service, and an outbound ticket webhook/outbox. The project emphasizes a fully runnable local mode, idempotent event handling, visible evidence, health checks, structured logs, and downstream failure preservation. Its synthetic thresholds are demonstrative only and require customer quality-team approval before any real deployment.
